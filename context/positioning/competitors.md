## Where PaidHR wins (supported by research)
- **Proof of remittance.** Remittance runs straight after payroll, with receipts. 2025 filings are reported as 100% on time. (company.md; customer-videos.md)
- **Employee financial wellness built in.** EWA, Loan Marketplace and USD wallet at no employer cost. No competitor in the research shows a wallet or a lender marketplace. NotchHR offers salary advances. (company.md; competitors.md)
- **Frontline access.** WhatsApp and USSD in Nigeria, Ghana and Kenya. (company.md)
- **Simplicity.** FAMASI chose Pade because others "didn't seem very simple to use". (customer-videos.md)
- **Channel.** Agencies and consultants can run white-label payroll. (company.md)

## Where PaidHR loses (supported by research)
- **Scale and proof volume.** SeamlessHR claims 2,000+ organisations and runs named-customer content. PaidHR has 400+ businesses and two 2023 videos. (competitors.md; customers.md)
- **Fully managed payroll or EOR.** Workpay sells EOR and outsourced payroll; PaidHR shows neither. (competitors.md)
- **Price floor.** Salario is free. (competitors.md)
- **App reliability perception.** 5 of 6 low Play Store ratings are about login or update bugs. (customers.md)
