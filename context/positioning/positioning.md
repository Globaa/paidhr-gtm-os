## Positioning statement
For Nigerian and African employers that have outgrown spreadsheets and manual remittances, PaidHR is the HR and payroll platform that files PAYE, pension, NHF and NSITF right after each payroll run. It also gives every employee access to their payslip and earned wages, on app, WhatsApp or USSD. Patchwork tools leave gaps between attendance, payroll and filings. PaidHR runs them on one record, and its 2025 filings are reported as 100% on time.
