# PaidHR Messaging

Built only from `context/docs/research/`. Claims usable in prospect copy are listed in `context/messaging/facts.md`. Last updated 2026-11-12.

## Core message
Payroll paid and statutory filings done on time, every run, with every employee able to see their pay and reach their earned wages, all from one platform built for how African businesses work.

Basis: company.md (Modules, Traction); voice.md ("Control for the business. Wellbeing for the people.").

## Words to use (from PaidHR and customer language)
- "on time", "every run", "remittance", "receipts", "evidence for the payment" (customer-videos.md)
- "simple" ("It's just a very simple interface to use") (customer-videos.md)
- "patchwork", "one record", "payslip", "earned wages", "financial wellbeing" (voice.md)
- "people operations", "statutory" (voice.md)

## Words and phrasing to avoid
- Everything in `knowledge-base/playbooks/copy-bans.yaml` (includes "free", "seamless", "streamline", "robust", "empower", "unlock", "comprehensive", em dashes, "!" in copy).
- "Pade" in new copy (old brand). (company.md)
- "fintech" to describe PaidHR (the CEO says it is an HR company). (voice.md)
- "#1" or "Africa's most robust" (not verified). (voice.md; company.md)
- "guarantee" of zero defaults. State the 2025 record as past results only. (company.md)
- Naming Bento or other competitors' failures. (competitors.md)
