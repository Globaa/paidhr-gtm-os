## Banned

### Banned angles
- Guarantees ("zero defaults guaranteed", "never miss a filing").
- Fear about competitors' failures, including naming Bento. (competitors.md)
- Price or "free" framing (no prices in research; Salario competes on free). (company.md; competitors.md)
- Ranking claims: "#1", "Africa's most robust". (voice.md; company.md)
- Calling PaidHR a fintech or a lender. (voice.md; company.md)
- Anything that diagnoses the prospect's team as failing (iron law 14 and the insult test).

### Banned words and phrasing (company-specific)
Global list: `knowledge-base/playbooks/copy-bans.yaml`. Company-specific additions:
- "Pade" (old brand)
- "fintech"
- "#1"
- "guaranteed" or "zero defaults" as a future promise
- Competitor brand names in cold copy (SeamlessHR, Workpay, NotchHR, Eazipay, Bujeti, Salario)

### Never in copy, under any circumstance
- Unapproved customer logos (list above).
- Deposit or licence claims. Research notes "doesn't have a license for holding deposits yet", date unconfirmed. (competitors.md)
- Play Store complaints or app bugs. (customers.md)
