# PaidHR: Customer Videos

Raw research, not conclusions. These are the case study videos on https://www.paidhr.com/customers, under the heading "BUSINESS SPOTLIGHTS". Checked 2026-11-12.

## How this was gathered
- **Videos found:** two unique YouTube embeds in the page HTML. `thPhRuim05g` is embedded twice and `1L3R7CMEmjI` once. No other video sources (Vimeo, Wistia, MP4) are on the page.
- **Transcripts:** YouTube's auto-generated English captions, pulled with `youtube-transcript-api`. No human-made captions exist for either video.
- **Metadata:** titles from YouTube oEmbed; descriptions, publish dates, lengths and view counts from each watch page.
- **Caption accuracy:** the auto-captions garble names. "paid" is the old brand **Pade** (both video descriptions say "Pade"). "Pharmacy" is **FAMASI**, "Hiroshi" is **Heroshe**. In the quotes below, corrections are in [square brackets]. Anything still unclear is marked **UNCLEAR**. The raw captions are in the appendix, unedited.
- **Brand at the time:** both videos are from 2023, when the product was called Pade. They describe Pade, not the current PaidHR feature set.

## Themes across both videos (INFERRED)
- **Remittance is the headline pain.** Both speakers lead with statutory remittances: doing them one by one, confirming them, waiting on them, and proving they were paid.
- **The buyers differ.** At FAMASI the CEO runs payroll herself (founder-led small team). At Heroshe an HR operations person does it. Both are plausible personas.
- **Simplicity is the reason they chose it.** FAMASI's CEO tried other tools and picked Pade because it "looked very simple".
- **Proof of payment matters.** Heroshe values the receipts as "evidence". FAMASI used to text each employee to confirm payment.
- **Small reach.** 427 and 169 views. These are proof assets for sales and the website, not reach drivers.

## Gaps
- Only two videos, both from 2023 and both under the Pade brand. No video from 2024 onward.
- No headcount, payroll value or money-saved figures in either video.
- Heroshe speaker's job title is unclear in the captions.
- The FAMASI video is embedded twice on the page; the second slot may have been meant for a third video (**INFERRED**).
