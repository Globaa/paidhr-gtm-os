## Direction (INFERRED from their own content)
- **INFERRED: moving upmarket.** In 2025, Accion described PaidHR as targeting "businesses with under 200 employees". In 2026, the homepage says "built for African enterprises" and LinkedIn says "fast-growing and enterprise organizations". The founder is also hiring sales staff for "SME and enterprise segments".
- **INFERRED: building a frontline and blue-collar wedge.** Evidence: the WhatsApp/USSD launch ("most of Africa's workforce does not" sit at a desk) and geo-referenced Time & Attendance.
