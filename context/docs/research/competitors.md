## SeamlessHR
- **LinkedIn description (exact, excerpt):** "SeamlessHR is the flagship workforce management platform from Seamless Technologies, the Operating System for Work in Africa." ([LinkedIn](https://ng.linkedin.com/company/seamlesshr-ltd) via treg, checked 2026-11-12)
- **Scope:** "everything organisations need to manage their people, from recruitment and onboarding to HR management, payroll, performance, learning, time and attendance, succession planning, and workforce planning." (same)
- **Frontline workers:** "Beyond office teams, SeamlessHR also supports frontline and blue-collar workforces through its Blue-Collar Worker Operations Platform (BWOP)". **INFERRED:** this overlaps directly with PaidHR's October 2026 WhatsApp/USSD launch aimed at workers who don't sit at a desk.
- **Scale claim:** "Trusted by more than 2,000 organisations across Africa" (same).
- **Slogan:** "Everything Works." Hashtags in use: #EverythingWorks #VoiceOfTheCustomer.
- **Recent content (Oct-Nov 2026):** heavy on performance management and appraisals, with named customer testimonials. They name Chinyere Azike ("Group Head of HR at Africa Plus Partners") and Ifeanyi Udeze, plus a SanlamAllianz Nigeria case study. **INFERRED:** they run named-customer proof at a pace PaidHR doesn't currently match.
- **Content marketing:** SeamlessHR dominates search for "best HR software Nigeria" with its own listicles. It also published payroll guidance on the 2026 tax reform ([seamlesshr.com/blog/nigerias-2025-tax-reforms-key-payroll-changes-for-2026/](https://seamlesshr.com/blog/nigerias-2025-tax-reforms-key-payroll-changes-for-2026/), search result) and a November 2025 payroll release note for the tax reform ([support.seamlesshr.com](https://support.seamlesshr.com/payroll-release-update-on-the-2025-nigerian-tax-reform-nita-november-2025)).
- **Funding:** $9M Series A extension in Jan 2025, led by Helios Digital Ventures with the Gates Foundation ([techeconomy.ng](https://techeconomy.ng/seamlesshr-raises-additional-9million/); [myjoyonline](https://www.myjoyonline.com/seamlesshr-funding-tally-lifts-to-us25million-with-helios-gates-foundation-investment-west-africa/), search summaries, checked 2026-11-12). Totals reported vary between $19M and $25M.

([LinkedIn](https://ng.linkedin.com/company/notchhr) via treg, checked 2026-11-12)
- **Slogan:** "NotchHR simplifies HR processes. Save time, save money and work efficiently."
- **Tone:** "NotchHR saves time, money and… headaches. The NotchHR experience is bye HASSLE! and welcome PEACE OF MIND!"
- **Product names:** the description references "MyXalary", which looks like a predecessor or sister product.
- **AI angle (Nov 2026):** "ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini can now connect to NotchHR, giving authorised users a faster way to access HR information and carry out supported tasks." **INFERRED:** this competes with PaidHR's PaidGuide AI assistant, announced 2026-11-10.

## Eazipay
- Positions itself as payroll infrastructure. Bundles "payroll, taxes, pensions and vendor payments in one workflow". Claims a full payroll run in about five minutes. Its CEO calls it "the No. 1 payroll software in Nigeria" (sponsored content, [techpoint.africa/?p=325018](https://techpoint.africa/brandpress/eazipay-is-quietly-fixing-one-of-africas-most-broken-business-systems-payroll/), search summary, checked 2026-11-12).
- Sells a Payroll API that banks and neobanks embed for their SME customers (same). **INFERRED:** this overlaps with PaidHR's Payroll API and white-label offer.
- Was honoured at TIL Awards for "modernising payroll and workforce payments", October 2026 ([techeconomy.ng](https://techeconomy.ng/tilawards-honours-eazipay-for-modernising-payroll-and-workforce-payments), search result).

## Bujeti
- Launched payroll in May 2026, built into its expense, vendor payment and tax filing dashboard. Coverage frames it as a response to the 2025 Tax Act ([TechCabal](https://techcabal.com/2026/05/21/bujeti-payroll-for-african-businesses/); [Techpoint](https://techpoint.africa/brandpress/bujetis-payroll-launch/), search summaries, checked 2026-11-12).
- Positioning headline: "Bujeti's Payroll Launch Bets That Payroll Belongs to Both HR and Finance" (Techpoint brand press).
- Founder interview headline: "The books should close themselves" ([techeconomy.ng](https://techeconomy.ng/the-books-should-close-themselves-bujetis-founders-on-payroll-launch-and-what-african-businesses-have-been-getting-wrong), search result).
- **INFERRED:** Bujeti sells payroll to the CFO, where PaidHR sells to HR.

## Salario
- "Salario Launches Free Payroll Solution to Help Nigerian SMEs Improve Tax Compliance" (March 2026, [Techpoint](https://techpoint.africa/?p=350481), search result, checked 2026-11-12).
- Free access model, no upfront licensing cost. Mostly brand-press coverage.
- **INFERRED:** sets a price floor for small SMEs.

## Bento Africa (defunct, but shapes buyer trust)
- Board suspended operations in early February 2025 after CEO Ebun Okubanjo resigned. Clients said payroll was not processed in the first week of February ([TechCabal](https://techcabal.com/2025/02/10/bento-africa-temporarily-shut-down/), search summary, checked 2026-11-12).
- Allegations of unremitted taxes and pension contributions; a Lagos State Inland Revenue Service probe was reported ([The Condia](https://thecondia.com/bento-ceo-resigns/); [Techpoint](https://techpoint.africa/insight/how-bento-africa-allegedly-scammed-customers/), search summaries).
- No relaunch found.
- **INFERRED:** this is the backdrop for PaidHR's "Zero-default compliance" and "100% on time" proof points. Buyers who lived through Bento may ask where remittance money sits. The Condia previously noted that PaidHR "doesn't have a license for holding deposits yet" ([thecondia.com/paid-hr-cross-border-payroll/](https://thecondia.com/paid-hr-cross-border-payroll/), search summary; date of that claim not confirmed).
