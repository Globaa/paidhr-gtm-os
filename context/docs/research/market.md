# Market: regulation and changes that create urgency

Raw research, not conclusions. Every fact has a source and the date it was checked. Anything inferred is marked **INFERRED**. Anything not found is marked **Not found**.

All checks were done on 2026-11-12.

**Caveat:** no primary government text was read. That means no gazetted Act and nothing from nrs.gov.ng or pencom.gov.ng. Figures come from law firms (DLA Piper, Forvis Mazars, KPMG), payroll vendors and news coverage. Verify against the primary text before using them in customer-facing claims.

## 1. Nigeria Tax Act 2025 and Nigeria Tax Administration Act 2025 (in force since 1 January 2026)

| Change | Detail | Source | Checked |
|---|---|---|---|
| Effective date | "These changes applied as at 1 January 2026, when The Nigeria Tax Act 2025 and the Nigeria Tax Administration Act 2025 among the other laws, took effect." | https://www.dlapiperafrica.com/en/nigeria/insights/2026/FAQs-on-payroll-taxes-and-employer-liability | 2026-11-12 |
| New PAYE bands | 0% on first ₦800,000; 15% on next ₦2,200,000; 18% on next ₦9,000,000; 21% on next ₦13,000,000; 23% on next ₦25,000,000; 25% above ₦50,000,000. The old range was 7% to 24% | DLA Piper (same) | 2026-11-12 |
| Minimum wage exemption | "Employment income is exempt where the employee's gross income from that employment is at or below the national minimum wage. The current minimum wage is ₦70,000 monthly." | DLA Piper | 2026-11-12 |
| CRA abolished, rent relief added | Rent relief is 20% of annual rent, capped at ₦500,000, "only granted when the employee proves rent payment with relevant document." | DLA Piper; https://www.forvismazars.com/ng/en/services/tax/nigeria-s-tax-reform-in-focus/rent-relief-allowance-under-2025-nigeria-tax-act | 2026-11-12 |
| Rent relief documents | "The employee should provide a receipt, lease agreement, or an employer-approved rent declaration by the company." | DLA Piper | 2026-11-12 |
| NHF now voluntary | "Private sector employers are to discontinue deductions for National Housing Fund (NHF) contribution, unless explicitly consented to by the employees." | DLA Piper | 2026-11-12 |
| Gratuity | "gratuity is now taxable, to be calculated with the new PAYE tax bands." | DLA Piper | 2026-11-12 |
| Severance | Tax-free threshold raised "from ₦10 million to ₦50 million." | DLA Piper | 2026-11-12 |
| Foreign-currency pay | Taxed "using the naira equivalent of the official CBN exchange rate at the time the income is paid as the PAYE base" | DLA Piper | 2026-11-12 |
| Benefits-in-kind | Valued at a cap of 5% of the asset's cost | DLA Piper | 2026-11-12 |
| Employer liability | The NTAA 2025 "expands employer liability under the PAYE framework"; administrative penalties of up to 40% are reported | https://gpa.net/blogs/africa/nigeria-higher-paye-penalties-prompting-tighter-payroll-compliance (search summary) | 2026-11-12 |
| Old systems give wrong answers | Payroll systems using the old bands produce incorrect deductions | DLA Piper (search summary) | 2026-11-12 |
| Annual PAYE returns | Due by 31 January each year. Penalty: ₦100,000 in the first month of default and ₦50,000 for each month after | https://www.legit.ng/business-economy/economy/1695169-fg-unveils-timelines-penalties-tax-regime-nigerians (search summary) | 2026-11-12 |
| Monthly remittance | December 2025 deductions were due 10 January 2026; then the 10th of each following month | legit.ng (search summary) | 2026-11-12 |
| CGT | Sage reports Capital Gains Tax abolished from 1 January 2026. **Conflicting with other sources; not verified** | https://communityhub.sage.com/za/sage-payroll-professional/f/announcements/261138/nigeria-payroll-tax-reform-changes-effective-january-2026 (search summary) | 2026-11-12 |
| Take-home pay | "Nigerian Salary Earners See Higher Take-Home Pay as Tinubu's Tax Reforms Kick In" | https://www.legit.ng/business-economy/economy/1695945-nigerian-salary-earners-higher-home-pay-tinubus-tax-reforms-bite | 2026-11-12 |

PaidHR's own claim: "Tax-law ready: Fully updated for Nigeria's new tax law, effective 1 January 2026 — before your first affected payroll run." ([paidhr.com](https://www.paidhr.com/), checked 2026-11-12)

## 2. Nigeria Revenue Service (NRS) and the mandatory Tax ID

| Change | Detail | Source | Checked |
|---|---|---|---|
| FIRS replaced | The Act "repeals the Federal Inland Revenue Service (FIRS) Act and establishes the Nigeria Revenue Service (NRS)" | Mondaq / law-firm summaries (search summary) | 2026-11-12 |
| Mandatory Tax ID | Joint public notice dated 18 May 2026 (NRS and the Joint Revenue Board, citing NTAA sections 6, 7 and 8). It makes a Tax ID mandatory for every taxable individual and business | https://allafrica.com/stories/202605190105.html ; https://www.legit.ng/business-economy/economy/1710885-nigeria-announces-mandatory-tax-individuals-businesses/ (search summaries) | 2026-11-12 |
| Banking tie-in | The Tax ID requirement is reported to extend to banking access | search summary | 2026-11-12 |
| Business Tax ID | One source says a company's CAC registration number now serves as its Tax ID; another says pre-2026 TINs remain valid. **Conflicting** | search summaries | 2026-11-12 |

**INFERRED:** every employee needs a Tax ID for PAYE filing. Employers that lack employee Tax IDs face a data-collection job that HR systems can help with.

| Lagos enforcement | PenCom and the NLC Lagos Council started enforcement from 1 June 2026, targeting employers who deduct contributions without remitting them | https://nairametrics.com/2026/05/19/pencom-nlc-to-begin-enforcement-against-pension-remittance-defaulters-in-lagos/ | 2026-11-12 |
| Criminal referral | PenCom and ICPC recovered about N3 billion from electricity-sector employers | https://nairametrics.com/2026/07/01/pencom-icpc-recover-n3-billion-in-unremitted-pension-contributions-from-electricity-sector-employers/ | 2026-11-12 |
| Remittance deadline | Within 7 working days after salaries are paid (Pension Reform Act 2014, s.11) | https://businessday.ng/news/article/pension-remittance-pencom-tells-employers-to-comply-or-face-sanctions/ (search summary) | 2026-11-12 |

**INFERRED:** a Pension Clearance Certificate is commonly required to bid for government and many corporate contracts. That makes the PCC block a direct revenue risk for vendors who fall behind. The PCC-for-contracts requirement was not verified in this pass.

## 6. Minimum wage review (live dispute)

| Fact | Source | Checked |
|---|---|---|

| Federal workers are demanding ₦300,000 | https://allafrica.com/stories/202609040021.html | 2026-11-12 |
