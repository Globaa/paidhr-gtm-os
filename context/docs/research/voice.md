## Observed patterns (INFERRED)
- **Two audiences in one line.** They write to the business and the employee at once: "Control for the business. Wellbeing for the people."
- **Contrasts.** Headlines often pair a pain with its opposite: "without the patchwork", "without the hundred spreadsheets", "Break Boundaries, Not Payroll".
- **"Paid" as a prefix** for product names: PaidHiring, PaidGuide, PaidRight, PaidLife.
- **Inconsistent proof numbers.** Currencies appear as 45+ and 49; businesses as 400+, 570+ and 100+; employees as 25,000+ and 43,000+.
- **Leftover errors on live pages.** Typos: "toady!" in "Choose PaidHR toady!" on customer pages, "qoute" on Heroshe, "whrere" in a 2026-11-12 LinkedIn post, "caccess" on X. One menu item ("Earned Wage Access") carries PaidHiring's subtitle.
