# PaidHR Brand

Built only from `context/docs/research/voice.md` unless noted. Quotes are PaidHR's own copy, exact. Last updated 2026-11-12.

## Tone of voice

| Trait | What it looks like | Example (exact) |
|---|---|---|
| Two audiences in one line | Business control plus employee wellbeing | "Control for the business. Wellbeing for the people." |
| Pain-versus-fix contrast | Names the old way, then the fix | "Hire without the hundred spreadsheets" / "People operations without the patchwork" |
| Plain and reassuring | Short claims about trust and timing | "Payroll that never misses payday" / "Compliance filed on time, automatically." |
| Employee empathy | Speaks to workers' money stress | "You shouldn't empty your paycheck just to survive till payday." |
| Warm, Nigerian, playful on social | Pidgin, humour, 💙 sign-off | "Chakam! 📸 Before you start complaining about our invoice." |
| Founder voice: data plus mission | Local stats, Africa-specific framing | "HR technology in Africa has largely been built for people who sit at a desk, but the reality is that most of Africa's workforce does not." |

## House rules for our copy
- No em dashes, even where PaidHR's own copy uses them. Some of their lines contain em dashes, so do not paste those verbatim into our copy.
- Do not carry their typos into our copy ("toady", "qoute", "whrere", "caccess").
- Use one figure per claim. Their own pages conflict (45+ vs 49 currencies, 400+ vs 570+ businesses). See context/messaging/facts.md.
