# PaidHR Campaign Strategy

**Status: DRAFT.** Built with the `campaign-strategy` skill on 2026-11-17 from `CLAUDE.md`, `context/` (ICP, personas, signal matrix, positioning, messaging, facts, case studies, GTM system) and `knowledge-base/playbooks/workflow-anatomy.md` (Mother Intent Catalog). No new web research or paid lookups were made.

**How to read this file**
- Signal IDs (U1, N1 and so on) refer to `context/icp/signal-matrix.md`. Segments: S1 funded tech/fintech, S2 frontline operators, S3 200+ employers, S4 payroll partners.
- **Volume per month is not counted anywhere in our research.** Every volume below is an INFERRED band, to be replaced with a real count after a test pull:
  - **High:** enough new accounts for a weekly send batch.
  - **Medium:** enough for a monthly batch.
  - **Low:** a small always-on trickle.
  - The one hard number we have: Nairametrics counted six major Nigerian startup deals in April 2026 (signal-matrix.md, U5).
- **Rank** = signal strength (1 to 5) + data ease (1 to 5), ties broken by volume. Signal strength comes from the Reason score in the signal matrix; data ease from its Data score, adjusted where noted.
- Angles are directions for `/campaign-copy`, not copy. Every claim and customer name in copy must come from `context/messaging/facts.md`, where **everything is still Pending approval**.
- Biggest shared risk: no test pull has checked that the job-post providers cover Nigerian boards like Jobberman (signal-matrix.md, "Before any build"). Campaigns 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 10 and 14 depend on it.

## Campaign details

### 1. HR or payroll hire, manual payroll skills (U1 + U2)
- **Signal and source:** a job post in the last 30 days for HR Manager, HR Officer, Payroll Officer or People Ops in Nigeria. The description mentions Excel payroll, PAYE or pension computation, or remittance to agencies. Data: LinkedIn Jobs, Indeed, Jobberman via treg `companies.jobs.search` or Clay "Find Jobs" (signal-matrix.md, U1, U2).
- **AI strategy:** Clay AI column reads the job description and pulls the payroll duties in the role's own words.
- **Angle (save time, mitigate risk):** the role being hired will own PAYE, pension, NHF and NSITF. PaidHR files them right after each run, with a receipt for each, so the new hire starts on people work. S2 version adds shift and attendance. Proof: Heroshe (facts.md, Pending).
- **Overview:** open with the role they are hiring and one duty quoted from the post. Describe the role, never diagnose the team. Run S1 and S2 at equal volume to test the industry question (icp.md). CTA: a short call before the hire starts. Different from 5: any HR or payroll hire, not only the first.

### 2. Frontline hiring wave (N1)
- **Signal and source:** 3 or more posts in 60 days for riders, drivers, warehouse, store or field sales staff. Data: Jobberman, HotNigerianJobs, Jobzilla, LinkedIn Jobs; posts counted per company in Clay (signal-matrix.md, N1). The threshold of 3 is a guess.
- **AI strategy:** count posts, extract the roles and locations.
- **Angle (save time, make retention cheaper):** the new staff work shifts, not desks. Attendance flows into payroll ("Every hour tracked, every hour paid", voice.md). Staff check payslips on WhatsApp or USSD. EWA at no cost to the business (facts.md, Pending).
- **Overview:** open with the roles they are hiring and where. Heroshe is the only logistics proof (2023). CTA: ask how pay for shift staff is tracked today.

### 3. Hiring in a second African country (U6)
- **Signal and source:** a Nigeria-HQ company posting jobs located in Kenya, Ghana or South Africa in the last 60 days. Data: as U1, with a location filter.
- **AI strategy:** none beyond the post's country and role.
- **Angle (save time):** one payroll for staff in several countries and currencies. The currency count (45+ vs 49) is unapproved, so leave it out until facts.md picks one.
- **Overview:** open with the country they are hiring in. CTA: ask how they plan to pay the first hire there. Check with PaidHR what local statutory filing it supports in each country before claiming it (signal-matrix.md, Kenya and Ghana held).

### 4. New HR or finance leader (New Hire, U3)
- **Signal and source:** HR Manager, Head of People, CFO, Finance Manager or Financial Controller who started in the last 90 days at an ICP company. Data: treg `apollo.people.search` ($0), confirmed with `leadmagic.x.job-change-detector` ($0.075) (signal-matrix.md, U3).
- **AI strategy:** pull start date, previous company and (for HR) whether the company has other HR staff.
- **Angle (mitigate risk, quick win):** the first filing cycle under them is the 31 January return, the first on the 2026 bands. Get remittance right from their first month. Finance version: deductions on the new bands and journals into the ERP.
- **Overview:** acknowledge the new role, reference the previous company only as context. Do not assume they will change tools (reason is INFERRED, signal-matrix.md). CTA: "worth comparing notes before January?"

### 5. First HR hire, no HR team yet (Creative stretch)
- **Signal and source:** campaign 1's job post, plus no current employee on LinkedIn with an HR or People title. Data: U1 plus Clay "Find People" at the company. Titles are reliably detectable, so this inverted signal is allowed by the skill rules.
- **AI strategy:** confirm no HR titles; find who runs payroll today (founder, COO, finance).
- **Angle (save time):** until now the founder or finance ran payroll; FAMASI's CEO got back about an hour each run (facts.md, Pending). Set up payroll and remittance before the new HR person arrives.
- **Overview:** send to the founder, not HR. Different from 1: targets the person handing payroll over.

### 6. Payroll service firms (Creative, non-ICP, N4)
- **Signal and source:** a Nigerian firm whose site or directory listing offers payroll administration, outsourcing or EOR. Timing: the firm is hiring payroll officers (U1). Data: Clutch, ConnectCiti, Finelib directories; site text via `crawl4ai` (signal-matrix.md, N4).
- **AI strategy:** read their services page; extract client types and services.
- **Angle (make money):** run payroll for clients under their own brand ("Make our payroll technology yours", voice.md). Every client moved to the 2026 bands once, in one engine.
- **Overview:** not a buyer of HR software, a reseller. **Blocked until PaidHR shares partner pricing and terms.** CTA: a partner call.

### 7. Recently funded startups (U5)
- **Signal and source:** Nigeria-HQ, seed to Series B round in the last 180 days. Data: treg `aviato.companies.funding_rounds` ($0.01); Nairametrics and TechCabal round-ups.
- **AI strategy:** round, date, investors; check for open HR or finance roles.
- **Angle (mitigate risk):** hiring after a round means more payroll and more filings. Set it up properly now. "Backed by Accion Venture Lab" builds peer trust (facts.md, Pending).
- **Overview:** low volume (six major deals in April 2026). Better as a secondary enrichment on 1 and 5, plus this small list.

### 8. New branch in another state (N2)
- **Signal and source:** a brand's Google Maps listings show a branch in a new Nigerian state since the last monthly run. Data: treg `google.serp.maps` ($0.00023 per call).
- **AI strategy:** name the new location; count states.
- **Angle (mitigate risk):** staff in a new state mean PAYE to that state's revenue service. PaidHR remits per state (signals.md, A2).
- **Overview:** open with the new branch. Useful for supermarkets and logistics hubs (S2, S3). Monthly snapshots must be stored, so the first run only builds a baseline.

### 9. 31 January PAYE return (No-AI)
- **Signal and source:** none; the date applies to every Nigerian employer (signal-matrix.md, N9). Base ICP list from Apollo.
- **AI strategy:** none. See "No-AI campaigns" below.
- **Angle (mitigate risk):** the first annual return on the 2026 bands is due 31 January 2027. Is payroll already on the new bands?
- **Overview:** time-boxed: send from now until mid-January, then stop. Also used as a timing line inside 1, 2 and 4.

### 10. Golden ICP stack (rep's 10-minute research)
- **Signal and source:** S1 company that raised in the last 180 days, is hiring HR or payroll, and has a post or staff in a second country. Data: 7 + 1 + 3 stacked.
- **AI strategy:** one paragraph tying all three: the round, the hire, the new country.
- **Angle (save time, mitigate risk):** growth on three fronts at once. One platform for payroll, filings and cross-border pay before the new HR lead arrives.
- **Overview:** the email a good rep writes by hand. Very small list; may be empty in some months. Test whether the stack beats 1 alone.

### 11. Lookalike of named customers (Lookalike, U8)
- **Signal and source:** same industry and headcount band as the seeds. S1 seeds: Mono, Risevest, Kredete, GetEquity, GO54. S2 seeds: Heroshe, ReelFruit. Data: Clay lookalike or company search.
- **AI strategy:** one line on what the prospect does that matches the seed.
- **Angle (save time):** "teams like yours" with FAMASI or Heroshe as proof. **Logo seeds are for list building only;** naming them needs approval (facts.md).
- **Overview:** no timing signal, so pair with 9 for urgency, or use as the base list for 15.

### 12. Headcount crossing 50 or up 20%+ (U4)
- **Signal and source:** LinkedIn headcount up 20%+ in 6 months, or band moving from 11-50 to 51-200. Data: treg `companyenrich.companies.workforce` ($0.049).
- **AI strategy:** none beyond the delta.
- **Angle (save time):** payroll by hand stops scaling as a team grows: FAMASI's "need for a more sustainable HR solution" (personas.md).
- **Overview:** S1 only. Frontline staff are rarely on LinkedIn, so S2 growth is undercounted.

### 13. Tax-reform content engagers (U7)
- **Signal and source:** HR or finance people at ICP companies who react to LinkedIn posts on PAYE or the Nigeria Tax Act, including competitor posts. Data: treg `linkedin.search.posts` then `linkedin.post.reactions`; `lead-signals` skill.
- **AI strategy:** topic of the post they engaged with (never quoted back).
- **Angle (mitigate risk):** the 2026 bands and what changed for payroll.
- **Overview:** never mention the engagement in copy. Best as a routing layer: move these accounts up the queue in 1 and 4.

### 14. ERP named in finance job posts (N3)
- **Signal and source:** finance job posts naming SAP, Oracle, Microsoft Dynamics or Sage. Data: as U1, description filter.
- **AI strategy:** extract the ERP from the post.
- **Angle (save time):** "Your ERP stays": payroll journals pushed in, not retyped (voice.md). Do not name an integration; none is confirmed (company.md).
- **Overview:** S3 CFO play. Hold until PaidHR has a 200+ customer story.

### 15. Creative Ideas from their website
- **Signal and source:** none; base list from 11 or Apollo filters.
- **AI strategy:** Claygent reads the site and writes three specific ideas, for example branches on the locations page leading to attendance across sites, careers pages with remote roles leading to cross-border pay, or a fast-growing team page leading to payroll self-service.
- **Angle:** "I had an idea for {{company_name}}" plus three bullets.
- **Overview:** needs a `prompt-builder` prompt and a QA pass so ideas only use approved product facts.

### 16. Appraisal season with Balanced Scorecard
- **Signal and source:** 200+ employers in Q4 appraisal season. PaidHR launched the Balanced Scorecard on 2026-10-26 and SeamlessHR ran appraisal campaigns in Oct-Nov 2026 (signals.md, A3). Detection: posts for performance or L&D roles (weak).
- **AI strategy:** none reliable.
- **Angle (save time):** run appraisals on the same record as payroll and HR.
- **Overview:** the window closes in December. Revisit for Q4 2027 with a better signal.

### 17. HR alumni of customer companies (Creative stretch)
- **Signal and source:** HR or payroll people now at an ICP company who previously worked at a logo customer. Data: Apollo people search on past company.
- **AI strategy:** past company and start date there.
- **Angle:** familiarity. **Never name the past company as a PaidHR customer** without approval, and do not assume they used it.
- **Overview:** best done with a PaidHR list of real past admin users (Champion/user migration). Ask PaidHR for it.

### 18. Accountants who run client payroll (Creative, non-ICP)
- **Signal and source:** accounting firms offering payroll to SME clients. PaidHR has a dedicated accountant page (company.md). Data: directories like those in 6; a professional body member list is not verified.
- **AI strategy:** read services page for payroll.
- **Angle (make money, save time):** run clients' payroll and filings in one place; refer or resell.
- **Overview:** overlaps with 6; start only if 6 works.

## No-AI campaigns

**31 January PAYE return (campaign 9)**
- **Why it works without AI:** a fixed date every Nigerian employer faces, and a yes/no question.
- **Core message:** the date; one line on the 2026 bands; one proof line from facts.md (tax-law ready); a soft yes/no question. Three sentences.

**Hiring for payroll (campaign 1, static version)**
- **Why it works without AI:** the job title alone is the reason, and it can be merged from the post without an AI column.
- **Core message:** "Saw you're hiring a {{job_title}}" plus one line on remittance with receipts, then a question.

## Front-end offers

1. **2026 PAYE band check.** Send one anonymised payslip; PaidHR recomputes it on the 2026 bands before the 31 January return. Value is shown before any purchase. Needs PaidHR's agreement and a person to run it (INFERRED offer, not in research).
2. **PaidRight salary benchmark.** PaidHR's own 2026 report from "25,000+ workers and 400+ employers" (signals.md). Offer the section for the prospect's industry. Content exists already; the figures are fuzzy in facts.md, so do not quote them.
3. **One payroll run on PaidHR.** Run one cycle with remittance and receipts as a pilot. Whether a trial exists is unknown (no pricing or trial in research).

## Recommendation: build campaign 1 first

**HR or payroll hire with manual payroll skills (U1 + U2), run in S1 and S2 at equal volume.**
- It has the highest signal strength and the highest score in the signal matrix (16 of 20). A company hiring someone to do payroll is the clearest "why now" we can detect.
- One build serves every segment; only keywords change. Campaigns 2, 3, 5 and 10 reuse the same job search, so the first build pays for four more.
- Running S1 and S2 side by side answers the top open question in the ICP (tech/fintech or logistics, retail and manufacturing) with reply data.
- Proof fits: Heroshe and FAMASI both describe the manual remittance work this role would do.

**Runner-up:** campaign 4 (New HR or finance leader). It is the cheapest to source ($0 people search) and does not depend on Nigerian job-board coverage. Build it first if the test pull below fails.

**Before building:**
1. Run one small test pull for U1 (Nigeria, last 30 days) to check the providers see Jobberman and LinkedIn Nigeria posts.
2. Get PaidHR to approve the facts this campaign needs (remittance proof, tax-law line, Heroshe and FAMASI) and name a sender.
3. Then run `/build-workflow-spec` on campaign 1.
